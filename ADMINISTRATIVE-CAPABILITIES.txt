Individual true/false values override IDENTITY_HUMAN_ADMINISTRATION_ENABLED.
Absent individual values inherit the compatibility group, whose default is false.
For independent activation keep the compatibility group explicitly false.
Bulk requires its own flag plus each operation flag and current Test classification.
It is bounded to 50 unique Humans and receipts. Each execution rechecks eligibility
inside the existing exclusive administrative transaction and returns its own result.
A completed idempotent receipt remains replayable after central metadata deletion.

IDENTITY_EMAIL_LINKING_ENABLED, IDENTITY_EMAIL_UNLINKING_ENABLED and
IDENTITY_PASSWORD_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED independently override the compatibility
IDENTITY_ACCOUNT_MANAGEMENT_ENABLED flag. Verified Email linking also requires
configured delivery and the host's trusted callback route. Existing native provider
link/unlink and profile paths remain separately available, with server-side owner,
recent authentication, proof, nonce, conflict and last-method checks unchanged.

Provider transfer and managed workload provisioning remain under their existing
separate flags. Cleanup-workload provisioning additionally requires central deletion
availability. Turning off deletion disables cleanup claims/completions, so pause and
review any pending cleanup before changing that flag. No test execution path is used
by the production-supported configuration, and no migration beyond 001–018 is added.

Safe startup audit receipts record effective boolean capabilities and configuration
hashes with actor system:runtime-configuration. They contain no secrets and preserve
transitions. New capabilities and previews do not assign purposes, lifecycle states,
provider ownership or access to existing Humans. Every mutation still requires an
explicit authorized decision, recent sensitive authentication and operation receipt.

Selected session references are SHA-256 hashes of centrally stored session IDs;
no bearer IDs or renewal tokens are returned. Preview binds action, Human, selected
reference, current evidence and exact confirmation. All-session revocation remains
supported. Revocation covers GAMA central access and renewal only; external product
sessions and externally stored records require their own verified integrations.

Central deletion deidentifies the Human, removes central sign-in credentials and
personal references, revokes its GAMA sessions, and retains canonical tombstones and
minimal audit/operation history. Business records, product catalogues, photographs,
shared assets and unrelated Humans are outside the deletion allowlist. Personal
product cleanup can be pending or unsupported and is never reported as completed
without scoped workload receipts. Last functioning administrator, sole live owner,
external ownership and outstanding Apple revocation obligations remain blockers.
Executed deletion, retirement and session revocation are not undone by flag/code
rollback. Reactivation applies only to suspended accounts and creates no old sessions.
